Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Peal is made by Daniel Ryman. It has no account and no sign-in, and we don't run our own servers or analytics. This policy explains what happens to data when you play.

What stays on your phone

Your progress, coins, streak, settings and reminder time are saved on your device only. We can't see them. Deleting the app removes them.

Ads

Peal is free and shows ads, served by Unity (Unity LevelPlay, with Unity Ads and ironSource Ads). To choose and measure ads, Unity's SDK collects data from your device, which can include: an advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking when iOS asks), the identifier for vendor, your IP address and rough location derived from it, device model and settings, and how you interact with ads. Unity's handling of this data is described in Unity's privacy policy.

In the UK, the EU, the EEA and Switzerland, Peal asks whether ads may be personalised. If you say no, ads are not personalised. You can change your answer in Peal's Settings at any time. You can also turn tracking off for all apps in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

If you buy Remove Ads, ads between puzzles stop. Ads you choose to watch for a hint may still be offered.

Purchases

Remove Ads is bought through Apple. We never see your payment details; Apple only tells the app that the purchase was made.

Game Center

If you use Game Center, your scores (today's time, your streak and the bells you've earned) are sent to Apple's Game Center leaderboards under your Game Center name. Apple's privacy policy covers this.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. Nothing is sent from a server, and you can turn them off in Peal's Settings or in iOS Settings.

Children

Peal is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. Questions: support@vinewoodvault.djr.li.
