How to play

Each row has every bell once. The top row is always 1, 2, 3, 4… From one row to the next, a bell stays put or swaps with a neighbour. Some bells are given; work out the rest.

Common questions

Is the daily the same for everyone? Yes. It changes at midnight where you are.

I bought Remove Ads on another device. Open Settings in Peal and tap Restore purchases.

Can I turn off the sound? Yes, Bells and Music each have a switch in Settings, and the silent switch mutes both.

Personalised ads. In the UK and EU you can change your choice in Settings at any time.
